Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026. Short, because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Usage statistics. We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read and which buttons are clicked. It sets cookies and records an approximate location, device type and the pages you visit. It does not tell us who you are.
  • What you send us. If you join the waitlist, email us or book a conversation, we receive the details you typed (for example your name and email address).
  • Calculator inputs. The Paycheck X-Ray and the other calculators run in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not run advertising trackers, and we never ask for your Social Security number, bank login or brokerage login.

Services that process data for us

Google Analytics (statistics), Payhip (community checkout and membership; payment details go to Payhip and its payment processors, never to us), Calendly (booking), and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Some links lead to products we recommend. When a link is an affiliate link, the other company may set its own cookie to record that you came from us. See the disclosure page.

Your choices

You can block Google Analytics with your browser settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to buildtomultiply@gmail.com.
