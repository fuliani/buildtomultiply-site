Paycheck X-Ray

Seven questions about your job. At the end, an estimate of how much money is being left behind every year, and what to do about each part.

3 minutesNo sign-upNothing leaves your browser
What is your gross annual salary, in dollars?

Base salary only, before taxes and before bonus.

How much of your salary do you contribute to your 401(k) today?

If you do not contribute, leave it at 0. If you are not sure, look for "401(k) contribution" in your HR portal.

Up to what percent does your employer match?

The match is employer money that only shows up if you contribute. It is in your benefits summary. If you do not know, choose "I don't know" and a conservative assumption is used.

Does your employer put money into an HSA for you?

An HSA (Health Savings Account) is the only American account that can be tax-free going in, while it grows, and coming out for qualified medical expenses. It only exists with a high-deductible health plan.

Does your company have an annual education or certification budget?

Many American employers offer one and almost nobody uses it. It is called tuition reimbursement or an education/learning stipend.

Have you ever negotiated pay at this company?

Hiring, promotion or a market adjustment. Any conversation where you asked for a number counts.

Do you know where your salary sits in the range for your level?

Every mid-size and large company has a range with a minimum, a midpoint and a maximum for each job level.
