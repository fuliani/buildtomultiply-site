The Roadmap

Seven stages, from paycheck to wealth.

It is not about picking stocks. It is about knowing which stage you are in and what to do now.

1

Earn

Raise what you take home: pay range, job level, negotiation, benefits.

2

Control

Create margin between what comes in and what goes out.

3

Protect

Emergency fund, insurance and expensive debt.

4

Build

Invest systematically: tax-advantaged accounts and consistency.

5

Own

Move beyond depending on a paycheck: stocks, real estate, a business.

6

Multiply

Let compounding, taxes and diversification work for you.

7

Live

Financial independence: time, family, generosity.
