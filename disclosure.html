How we make money

You should know who pays us before you take a recommendation from us.

Our own products

Build to Multiply earns money from its paid community and, in the future, from its own courses and tools. Most of what we publish is free.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site and on our social profiles are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through one of them, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked where they appear.

What we will and will not recommend

  • We only list tools that solve a problem our readers actually have, such as sending money to Brazil, filing taxes on a visa or budgeting in dollars.
  • We do not accept payment to recommend investments, brokerages, crypto exchanges, credit cards or loans.
  • A commission never changes what we say about a product. If a free option is good enough, we say so.

Education, not advice

Educational content. Build to Multiply does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax or legal advice. The calculators show estimates based on what you type and are not predictions or guarantees.
