What you get every month

1

A live review of your own case

This is why the community exists.

Once a month, members bring their own pay stub, benefits summary or offer letter, and we go through it live. You see your case and you learn from everyone else's. Almost nobody offers this, because almost nobody spent twenty years deciding pay ranges, job levels and benefits packages from the employer's side.

2

Live Q&A every week

45 minutes, recorded.

You send the question ahead, I answer it live. If you cannot make it, you get the recording. Over time this becomes the most complete answer library on the American paycheck.

3

Clinics at the right time of year

When the decision actually happens.

Open enrollment in November, when you have two weeks to choose a plan and do not understand the options. RSUs and year-end in December. Raises and the review cycle in the first quarter. Every clinic is live and recorded.

4

The whole library

Including what is not public.

Every spreadsheet on the site plus the ones that only exist here: offer comparison with equity, a total compensation calculator, and prep for the raise conversation.

5

A genuinely small group

Access only exists if it is small.

Brazilians in the same situation, with the same deadlines and the same questions. The seat cap is not a sales trick: it is what lets me look at each person's case.

What is not here

Buy or sell recommendations, suggested portfolios, trade signals, or anything that requires a license I do not hold. This is education about how the American pay and benefits system works. When your situation calls for a licensed professional, I will say so instead of selling you something.

A note on language

This site is in English because that is the language of your paycheck. The community itself, the live reviews and the Q&A, runs in Portuguese, because money questions are easier to ask in your own language.
